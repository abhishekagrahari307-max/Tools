PDF merger

Add the PDFs in the order you want them. Use the arrows to reorder, then download one combined document. Pages keep their original size and quality.

    Need to drop pages instead? Use organise pages after merging.

    Split PDF

    Pull pages out of a PDF. Keep several ranges at once, or make one file per page — handy for forms, invoices and answer sheets.

      The original file is untouched — you only download the new PDFs.

      PDF to JPG

      Turn each PDF page into a picture you can post, print or paste into a document. Choose JPG for photos or PNG for crisp text.

        Quality88%

        Need editable text instead? Try PDF to Word or PDF to text.

        Images to PDF

        Drop photos or scans and get one tidy PDF. Fit-to-page keeps the original picture size; A4 gives every image a full page.

          Have a PDF already? Compress it or merge it with others.

          PDF compressor

          Two honest options. Clean up rewrites the file without unused objects — good for exported documents, harmless to text. Shrink to a size turns each page into a picture and searches for the sharpest settings that fit your limit.

            If the result is not smaller, keep your original — the tool always tells you the real before/after size.

            PDF to Word

            Reads the text of each page and writes a real .docx you can open in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice. Page breaks are kept. Scanned PDFs without a text layer cannot be converted here.

              Need the words as plain text? PDF to text gives you a .txt file.

              PDF to text

              Pulls the selectable text out of a PDF into a .txt file, page by page. Scans without a text layer need OCR, which this offline page cannot do.

                Want a Word file instead? Use PDF to Word.

                Organise pages

                Type the pages in the order you want them. Leave a page out and it is deleted. The rest keep their original quality.

                  Leave the box empty to keep every page in its current order.

                  Rotate PDF

                  Turn the whole document or only some pages. Rotation is stored in the file, so the page stays sharp and searchable.

                    Reordering as well? Organise pages changes the order.

                    Add page numbers

                    Stamp page numbers where you want them — bottom centre by default, or a corner. Choose plain numbers, “3 / 12” or “Page 3”.

                      For a name or logo across the page, use Watermark PDF.

                      Watermark PDF

                      Write a word or a name and it is drawn across every page at the angle and strength you pick. Useful for drafts, samples and internal copies.

                        Strength18%

                        Watermarks use Latin letters — for Hindi text, flatten the page first with PDF to JPG.

                          Shrink photos for a form, a website or a chat. “Fit inside” keeps the aspect ratio, “Exact size” stretches to the numbers you type, and “Percent” scales the original.

                            Compress to a size

                            Forms often ask for “under 50 KB”. Type the limit and the tool searches quality and dimensions until the file fits — and still opens normally.

                              Crop image

                              Cut a picture down to the numbers you type. Coordinates start at the top-left corner; anything outside the picture is clamped so you always get a valid file.

                                Passport sizes are already cropped for you — try Passport photo.

                                Rotate & flip

                                Turn a sideways phone photo the right way up, or mirror a scan. The picture is redrawn at full size, never stretched.

                                  Output is PNG so nothing is blurred by extra JPG encoding.

                                  Watermark image

                                  Put your name, shop or “©” line onto photos before you share them. Text is outlined so it stays readable on light and dark pictures.

                                    Photos with transparency often look black in forms and apps. This flattens them onto white — or any colour you type — without touching the picture.

                                      Quality92%

                                      Photo + signature

                                      Many forms want the photo and the signature in a single image. Drop the photo first, then the signature, and they are stacked with a clean white gap.

                                        Output is one PNG with a white background, ready to upload.

                                        Passport photo

                                        Crop any picture to a standard passport or visa size at print resolution (300 dpi by default) and download a JPG that photo shops accept.
