Why nothing gets uploaded

On-device processing

Audio is decoded with the Web Audio API, video frames are drawn to a canvas, PDFs are edited with PDF-Lib and qpdf compiled to WebAssembly, and OCR runs on a bundled Tesseract build. Your bytes stay in this tab.

No CDN, no tracking

Every library is served from this folder. There is no analytics, no account, no cookie wall and no third-party request while you work — the pages keep working once loaded, even offline.

Heavy engines load on demand

The OCR models, PDF engines and Hindi font live in pixora/engines/ and are fetched only when you open a tool that needs them, so the studio opens in seconds instead of carrying 11 MB everywhere.

Good to know

  • Serve these pages over http(s) (GitHub Pages does this). Opening the HTML straight from disk blocks the on-demand engine files.
  • Video previews and exports use your browser's own codecs; Chrome or Edge will export MP4, other browsers fall back to WebM.
  • Nothing is permanent: reloading clears the workspace, so download finished files before you close the tab.
